About ETHIQ

Small Questions. A Bigger You.

Think. Reflect. Decide.

ETHIQ is a digital ethics reflection card project created by Group 1 for OLVAL02 – Ethics at ICCT College Cainta. It encourages students to pause, reflect, and think critically about moral choices encountered in everyday life.

The collection contains 300 reflection cards across six categories—Self, Relationships, Dilemmas, Digital Life, Moral Compass, and Justice. Each card includes an English and Tagalog question and an inspirational message.

Personal Progress: ETHIQ is designed so a future Google sign-in option can let each user save their own answers, reflections, and progress to their own Google account/Google Sheet. This is intended as a personal-data feature, separate from the public question library.
